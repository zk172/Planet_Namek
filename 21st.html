21st Tenkaichi Budoukai

Episodes: 19-28
Manga Volumes: 3-5
Date: May 7, 750 A.D.
Total Entrants: 137
Finalists: 8
Winner: Jackie Chun (Muten Roshi)
Runner-up: Goku
Prize Money: 500,000 zeni





The 21st Tenkaichi Budoukai takes place about three months after Goku thwarts Lord Pilaf's plans for world domination.

The 8 finalists were decided by an eight block preliminary battle round. Each block consisted of approximately 17 fighters. A total of 129 preliminary matches took place. The preliminary rounds were held in a room concealed from the Tenkaichi Budoukai spectators. The 8 finalists fought in an open square in the center of a small stadium.

During the first round of the finals, Kuririn is scheduled to face Bacterian, Yamucha is to face Jackie Chun, Lanfan will face Namu, and Goku will battle Giran.
